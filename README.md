# Bright-TV

🧠 Project Overview
This project analyzes TV channel viewership data to uncover patterns in:
•	📺 Content performance
•	⏰ Viewing behavior (time & day)
•	🌍 Regional distribution
•	⏳ Engagement duration
The goal is to provide actionable insights to improve user retention, content strategy, and platform growth.
 
🎯 Objectives
•	Identify top-performing channels
•	Understand when users watch content
•	Analyse regional engagement trends
•	Evaluate viewing duration (retention)
•	Provide business recommendations

🔍 Key Insights
📺 Content
•	Sports & live events dominate (SuperSport, ICC events)
•	Music channels (Channel O, Trace TV) perform strongly
•	Kids content shows stable demand
 
⏰ Timing
•	Peak: Afternoon
•	Strong: Morning & Evening
•	Weak: Late night
 
📅 Weekly Trends
•	Weekdays: ~70% of views
•	Peak days: Friday & Saturday
•	Lowest: Monday
 
🌍 Geography
•	Gauteng leads (~36%)
•	Western Cape second (~18%)
•	Data quality issues (missing regions)

⏳ Engagement (Critical Insight 🚨)
•	92% of sessions are short
•	Very low high-duration engagement
•	Indicates major retention problem
 
🚨 Core Problem
The platform attracts users but fails to keep them engaged.
 
💡 Recommendations
🔥 Retention First
•	Improve first 30–60 seconds of content
•	Focus on watch time, not just views
📺 Content Strategy
•	Invest in sports & live events
•	Expand music & youth content
 
🤖 Product Improvements
•	Autoplay next content
•	“Continue watching”
•	Smart recommendations
 
⏰ Scheduling
•	Focus on afternoons, Fridays, Saturdays
•	Avoid major releases on Mondays
 
⚙️ Data & Operations
•	Fix missing region data
•	Resolve transmission issues
